How a Session Works

Each session runs about 60 minutes, over Zoom or in person in the NYC area, and follows a simple, repeating rhythm:

  1. 1
    We meet. Sessions are built around whatever your student is working on that week — we start from their current assignment or goal and work through it together.
  2. 2
    You get a recap. After each session, you'll receive a short summary of what we covered, plus any out-of-session work to keep the momentum going before we meet again.
  3. 3
    We meet again. We pick up from the recap, so nothing gets lost between sessions.

What a Session Can Cover

  • Reading, annotating, and analyzing literature, and prepping for class discussion
  • Understanding an assignment and planning an approach before drafting
  • Drafting, revising, and editing — at any stage of a paper
  • Targeted drafting and revision exercises to build writing skills in real time
  • Research papers, literary analysis, and writing-focused coursework across subjects

Logistics

  • Scheduling: Most students meet once a week at a recurring time. Some meet as often as twice a week, others biweekly or as needed — whatever fits your student's workload and goals.
  • Format: Zoom or in person in the NYC area.
  • Payment: Zelle (914-960-4398) preferred; Venmo (@bonannowriting) also accepted.
